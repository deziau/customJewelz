    Photos

    The first photo is the one customers see in the collection. Upload one photo per colour, in the same order as the colours below, and the picture follows whichever colour the customer picks. Photos are shrunk to fit. Photos are used in the collection only — the design area uses the stickers below.

    Stickers for the design area *

    A sticker is the same piece cut out on a transparent background — a PNG saved with no backdrop. It is what customers drag onto their canvas, so pieces overlap like real ones instead of covering each other with squares. Every component needs at least one. Upload one sticker per colour, in the same order as the colours below, and the sticker follows the colour they picked. The chequerboard behind each tile is see-through: if you cannot see it, that file still has a background. Crop a sticker tight to the piece: the design area draws it at exactly the width and height entered above, and any empty margin is drawn along with it. For a component sold by length, the sticker is repeated down the strand, so say above how many centimetres of it the picture shows — that is what keeps the links their real size whatever length is cut.

    How it hangs in Create

    If the bangle has its own loops, click each one on the picture — a charm hangs from every loop marked. Click a marker again to remove it. With none marked, charms are spaced along the band.

    Click the loop on the sticker — the jump ring goes through it and the charm hangs from that point.
